The organization I chose to focus on is Sensata Technologies. Sensata operates in the manufacturing and technology industries, primarily producing sensors and other devices that allow equipment to collect and communicate data. Sensata’s website can be found at https://www.sensata.com/.

Sensata utilizes IoT through sensors that collect information from equipment and manufacturing processes. These sensors can monitor physical conditions and turn those measurements into data that can be transmitted and analyzed. This is especially valuable in manufacturing because companies can use the information to monitor equipment, improve processes, and potentially identify problems before they become more serious.

One major advantage I see for Sensata is the growth of AI. The large number of data points produced by IoT sensors could make manufacturing processes more precise. As AI systems become better at analyzing large amounts of information, the data collected by Sensata’s sensors could become increasingly valuable. However, this could also create a business challenge. It is relatively easy to justify purchasing a sensor for a proven manufacturing process where the customer already understands its purpose. It is much harder to convince a company to purchase additional sensors because the data might eventually be useful for AI. Experimental technology sounds great until a company has to justify the additional operating costs.

I think interoperability is ultimately the biggest disadvantage for Sensata when utilizing IoT. Sensata’s sensors need to work across equipment produced by many different manufacturers. Design constraints such as sensor size, placement, power requirements, and connectivity could become major problems. A sensor that works perfectly on one company's equipment might not be ideal for another company's equipment, even if both machines are performing essentially the same manufacturing process.

Because of this, I think Sensata would benefit from a modular approach. The company could leverage the same core sensor technology while providing different physical “cases” or configurations depending on the equipment being monitored. This would allow Sensata to reuse its underlying technology while adapting things such as the sensor's shape, mounting location, power connection, and communication method. Instead of attempting to design one sensor that works everywhere, the core technology could remain consistent while the way it integrates with each manufacturer's equipment changes.

Ultimately, IoT gives Sensata an opportunity to provide manufacturers with increasingly useful data, especially as AI improves the ability to analyze that information. At the same time, collecting data is only useful if the sensors can actually be integrated into real manufacturing equipment. For Sensata, solving the physical and technical interoperability problem may be just as important as improving the sensors themselves.
